SNIPR SHOPPING GUIDE

How to buy electronics for less in Australia

A low advertised price is only useful when the product meets your needs. Compare the exact model, warranty and total delivered cost before deciding.

Compare the exact model

Check the model number, storage, generation and included accessories. Products with similar names can have different specifications.

Understand the warranty

Identify the actual seller, especially on marketplaces. Check Australian consumer guarantees, repair arrangements and return conditions before purchasing.

Calculate total cost

Include delivery, required accessories, installation and any subscription fees. Avoid treating conditional cashback as a guaranteed discount.

Before you buy

  • Exact model number
  • Australian compatibility
  • Actual marketplace seller
  • Total delivered cost
  • Warranty and return conditions
