SNIPR SHOPPING GUIDE

A practical guide to Australian grocery savings

Shopping by unit price and planning purchases around actual household consumption can be more useful than chasing headline discounts.

Use comparable unit prices

Compare products per kilogram, litre or 100 grams. Different package sizes can make promotional prices misleading.

Avoid unnecessary bulk purchases

A multipack can cost less per unit but still increase household spending if food expires or goes unused.

Consider the whole shopping basket

Travel, delivery and minimum-order charges can offset the savings from visiting multiple retailers.

Before you buy

  • Unit price
  • Expiry date
  • Storage capacity
  • Minimum order
  • Travel and delivery cost

General shopping guidance. No specific product testing or retailer endorsement is implied.
