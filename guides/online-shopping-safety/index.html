SNIPR SHOPPING GUIDE

Safer online shopping in Australia

Before following an unfamiliar deal, identify the seller and confirm the offer on an appropriate retailer website.

Check the destination

Inspect the website address rather than relying only on logos. A familiar brand name in a listing is not proof that the destination belongs to that retailer.

Review payment options

Use payment methods with suitable consumer protections. Avoid sending money to unfamiliar personal accounts solely because an offer is urgent.

Keep transaction evidence

Save the advertised terms, order confirmation and payment receipt. These may be useful if a dispute arises.

Before you buy

  • Retailer identity
  • Correct website address
  • Contact details
  • Payment protections
  • Return conditions
