SNIPR SHOPPING GUIDE

Comparing home appliances before buying

The purchase price is only one part of appliance ownership. Capacity, energy use and installation can materially affect value.

Check dimensions and capacity

Measure the space, doorways and required clearance. An appliance that cannot be installed is not a useful bargain.

Estimate ongoing running costs

Compare annual electricity or water consumption using the applicable energy information and your own utility tariff.

Confirm delivery and installation

Check whether delivery includes stairs, installation, packaging removal or disposal of an old appliance.

Before you buy

  • Dimensions
  • Energy consumption
  • Delivery restrictions
  • Installation cost
  • Service and repair options
