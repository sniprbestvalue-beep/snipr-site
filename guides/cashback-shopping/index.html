SNIPR SHOPPING GUIDE

How to assess cashback offers

Cashback can reduce the eventual cost of a purchase, but payment may depend on tracking, approval and compliance with offer conditions.

Check eligibility

Read the eligible product categories, customer restrictions and cashback caps. Some purchases or coupon codes may invalidate cashback.

Distinguish cash paid from expected cashback

The checkout amount is what you initially pay. Pending cashback should not be treated as money already received.

Keep purchase records

Retain order confirmations and relevant offer conditions in case a tracking issue needs to be investigated.

Before you buy

  • Cashback exclusions
  • Cashback cap
  • Coupon eligibility
  • Approval period
  • Withdrawal conditions
