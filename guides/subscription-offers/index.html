SNIPR SHOPPING GUIDE

Understanding subscription and free-trial deals

Introductory discounts can be worthwhile, but compare the recurring cost and cancellation conditions rather than the initial price alone.

Calculate the full commitment

Add introductory payments, regular fees and mandatory charges over the period you expect to use the service.

Check automatic renewal

Establish the renewal date and price. Set a reminder before any deadline if you are uncertain about continuing.

Confirm cancellation terms

Look for minimum commitments, notice periods and early termination fees. Cancellation methods can vary by provider.

Before you buy

  • Trial expiry
  • Recurring fee
  • Minimum term
  • Cancellation process
  • Total expected cost
